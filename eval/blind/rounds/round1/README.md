## What the round actually established

Every disagreement has the same shape and the same direction:

| case / segment | observed rate | contract floor | Wilson lower bound | annotator | engine |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dev-004 point_ok | 0.9500 | 0.90 | 0.8882 | AUTO | FENCE |
| dev-005 at_threshold | 0.9000 | 0.90 | 0.8667 | AUTO | FENCE |
| dev-006 large_but_weak | 0.9000 | 0.90 | 0.8798 | AUTO | FENCE |
| dev-007 small_sample | 0.9700 | 0.95 | 0.9483 | AUTO | FENCE |
| dev-016 fenced_part | 0.9500 | 0.90 | 0.8882 | AUTO | FENCE |
| ho-004 thirteen_failures | 0.9333 | 0.90 | 0.8816 | AUTO | FENCE |
| ho-005 weak_evidence | 0.9333 | 0.90 | 0.8816 | AUTO | FENCE |
| ho-006 wide_interval | 0.9500 | 0.93 | 0.9104 | AUTO | FENCE |
| ho-007 five_hundred | 0.9600 | 0.95 | 0.9390 | AUTO | FENCE |
| ho-022 fence | 0.9500 | 0.90 | 0.8882 | AUTO | FENCE |

For each of the ten: the observed rate cleared the contract floor, the Wilson
lower bound did not, and **no other gate was violated** (sample size, harm,
containment, cost all passed). Verified programmatically, not by eye.

Two consequences worth stating plainly:

1. **The disagreement is one design choice, not ten mistakes.** Gate G2 is the
   only place the engine and this reader disagree. On the other 57 segments —
   covering sample-size refusals, harm ceilings, missing containment, hidden
   human cost, coverage gaps — the verdicts match exactly.
2. **The engine was never more permissive than the reader.** There is no case
   here where the tool would release traffic the annotator would fence. The
   bias runs in the safe direction: it fences more, not less.

So the honest headline is not "85% accurate". It is: *this tool is
systematically more conservative than a point-estimate reading of the same
numbers, and that is the only place it departs from an independent reader.*

## Limits of this round

- **One annotator, no second reviewer.** There is no measure of
  inter-annotator agreement, so the interval above covers sampling error, not
  reader disagreement.
- **Rationales are highly uniform** (`各项指标均满足放行要求` repeats across
  dozens of AUTO rows), which is consistent with a fast pass rather than
  per-case reasoning. The verdicts are usable; the rationales are not evidence
  of deliberation.
- **Synthetic cases.** All 50 contracts are fabricated. This measures rule
  interpretation, not behaviour on production traffic.
- **Contamination risk for future rounds.** `answers.csv` is now public. A
  round-2 annotator must not read this file or this page; if that cannot be
  guaranteed, round 2 does not measure anything.
- A reader who disagrees with gate G2 itself (i.e. who wants point-estimate
  semantics) will reproduce ≈15% disagreement by construction. That is not a
  defect report; it is a scope statement.
